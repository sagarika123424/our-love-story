12 September 2026

The girl by the window

You came to my house, and I was sitting by the window, looking at you while trying to hide as much as possible. I genuinely thought I was being very clever.

Except you could see me through the mirror. And when I finally came to speak to your father, you looked at me. Twice. I remember.

I don't know exactly what was going through your mind. I only know that what could have been a formal meeting became the first page of something I now hold so close to my heart.

Imagine hiding from the very person you were there to meet. That was me. 😂❤️

We went out with our families, everything felt nice, and then came the yes. Our families said yes. We said yes. And just like that, there was an us.

13 September 2026 · Around 8 PM

A cold evening & one enormous gulab jamun

We met again at your Didi's place for dinner. After that, we went for a long walk. The weather was cold, the wind was lovely, and the evening felt so easy.

We sat in the park, chilled, and spent time together. No grand plans. No big gestures. Just us, getting a little more comfortable with each other.

And then you made me eat that HUGE gulab jamun.

Of all the things I could remember from those days, this tiny, silly moment found its own place in my heart. Maybe because it felt so natural. Maybe because I was happy and didn't need to explain why.

Dear husband: gulab jamuns are now officially part of our relationship history. No amendments allowed. 😂

I hope years from now we still go for walks in cold weather, share food, laugh at the smallest things, and make ordinary evenings feel special.

14 September 2026

"Welcome home."

Our roka. Our families. Our new beginning. A day full of feelings that are difficult to put into words.

And then you said, "Welcome home."

Maybe those words sounded simple to you. But I keep remembering them, because home can be more than a place. It can be a feeling. It can be the comfort of knowing there is someone you are slowly building a life with.

You left for Bangalore around five that evening. But those words stayed with me.

Three little words. A memory I want to keep for a long time. ❤️

Bangalore · Our little moments

Getting to know you

Those days we spent together in Bangalore gave us more little memories, more laughter, and more chances to discover the people behind our first impressions.

And you trying to kiss me when the family was around? Sir, you really had your own agenda. 😂

I like remembering those moments because beneath the formalities of an arranged marriage, we were simply two people attracted to each other, getting closer and finding our own little world.

The best parts of a love story are often the bits that would make no sense to anybody else.

✦ ♡ ✦

But this is only chapter one.

We have so much left to discover about each other. More places to see, more memories to make, more ordinary days that will somehow become our favourites. I don't need to know exactly what our future looks like. I just want to keep building it with you.

To the boy who became my home ❤️

You spend your days teaching machines to learn, recognising patterns, and making predictions. But I hope you know that getting to know you is one of my favourite things to do.

I want to know the engineer who gets excited about his work, the person behind the screen, the boy who noticed me through a mirror, and the husband who welcomed me home.

I want us to have a real story. Not a perfect one. One where we keep talking, keep laughing, keep understanding each other, and keep making space for each other as we grow.

If our life together were a project, I wouldn't want to rush to the final version. I would want to build it with you, one day at a time.

More walks. More chai. More gulab jamuns. More silly jokes. More adventures. More little moments that belong only to us.

I'm glad you found me before I really found you. I'm glad I looked through that window. I'm glad our families brought us together. And I'm so glad that we get to write the rest of this story ourselves.

You may build artificial intelligence, but you make my real life beautiful.

Here's to us, to our beginning, and to everything we haven't lived yet.

With all my love,
Your wife. ❤️
